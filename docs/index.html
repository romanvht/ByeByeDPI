Everything you need, nothing you don't

Sidestep is a redesigned fork of ByeByeDPI, made to be simple from the first tap.

  • One tap

    One big button, a clear status, and a hint for what to do next.

  • Finds what works

    The strategy finder tests ready-made strategies on your network, scores them and applies the best one.

  • Blocked apps

    Sees which country you are in, lists the apps blocked there and installs them in one tap.

  • Stays on your device

    Your traffic is not sent to any server. Everything runs on your phone.

  • VPN or proxy

    Route all apps, all except a few, only the ones you choose, or use a local SOCKS5 proxy.

  • Android TV

    Works on TVs and TV boxes, controlled with a remote.

  • 18 languages

    From Arabic to Vietnamese, including right-to-left layouts.

  • Looks at home

    Light and dark themes, and colors from your wallpaper on Android 12 and newer.

Blocked apps, one tap away

Sidestep works out which country you are in and shows the apps that are blocked there, such as YouTube and Discord in Russia, or Discord and Roblox in Türkiye. Tap Install: it downloads the app and opens Android's installer.

Checked before installing

Apps come from their developers' official sites, from F-Droid or, for apps that are only on Google Play, from the APKPure mirror. A file from the mirror is installed only if it carries the developer's own signature.

Questions and answers

Is Sidestep a VPN?

No. It uses Android's VPN feature to route traffic on your device, but nothing is sent to a remote server. It does not hide your IP address or encrypt your traffic.

Is it free?

Yes. Sidestep is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license. No ads, no account, no subscription.

Will it unblock everything?

It gets around blocking based on deep packet inspection, which many providers use. Sites that are blocked by their IP address need a VPN instead.

Which devices does it run on?

Phones, tablets and TVs with Android 5.0 or newer.

I use ByeByeDPI. Can I keep my settings?

Yes. Export them in ByeByeDPI and import the file in Sidestep. The two apps can be installed side by side; keep only one of them connected.

Where do I download it?

From the Releases page on GitHub. Pick the APK for your device, or the universal one if you are not sure, and open it.
